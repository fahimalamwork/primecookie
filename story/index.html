Our story

From one kitchen to your sweet tooth.

Primé Cookie started the way the best bakeries do: friends asking for “just one more batch,” and then asking again.

By day, our founder is a solutions engineer, which means she solves problems for a living. Her favorite solution is brown butter. Every cookie is portioned by hand, rested for deeper flavor, and baked until the edges crackle and the middle stays soft.

About the name

It's pree-may.

In French, primé means award-winning. We think every cookie should earn the title. If you say “prime,” that's fine too. We'll still give you cookies.

How we bake

Four steps. No shortcuts.

  1. Brown the butter

    Slowly, until it smells like toffee. It's most of the personality.

  2. Rest the dough

    A full 48 hours. Good things take time, and so do cookies.

  3. Bake in small batches

    A few trays at a time, so every one gets our full attention.

  4. Box them warm

    What happens on the drive home is between you and the box.
