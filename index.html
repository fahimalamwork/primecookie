Our story

From one kitchen to your sweet tooth.

By day, our founder is a solutions engineer. Before that she was in QA, professionally finding everything that could go wrong. Turns out that's the perfect training for baking: every cookie is portioned by hand, rested for deeper flavor, and tested until the edges crackle and the middle stays soft. Nothing ships until it passes.

Why “Pree-may”? In French, primé means award-winning. We think every cookie should earn the title.
