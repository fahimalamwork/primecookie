Our story

From one kitchen to your sweet tooth.

Prime Cookie started the way the best bakeries do: friends asking for “just one more batch.” Every cookie is portioned by hand, rested overnight for deeper flavor, and baked until the edges crackle and the middle stays soft.

Why “Pree-may”? In French, primé means award-winning. We think every cookie should earn the title.
