FAQ

Good questions.

When do you launch?

Soon. Join the list above and you'll hear first, along with pop-up dates and pre-orders.

How do I order?

At launch you'll be able to pre-order a box online and pick a pickup or local delivery slot. Details are coming soon.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes, with a little notice. Birthdays, office treats, thank-yous, or a Tuesday.

How long do the cookies stay fresh?

Several days in an airtight container. In our experience, they rarely get the chance.

Can I freeze them?

Yes. Wrap them well, then warm one in the oven for a few minutes and it's nearly fresh-baked.

What about allergies?

Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs and tree nuts, so we can't promise anything is allergen-free. Ask us about specific needs.

Is it “prime” or “pree-may”?

Pree-may. Primé is French for award-winning. But we'll answer to anything if you're ordering.
